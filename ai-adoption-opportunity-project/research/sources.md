## How to Read This List

Sources are grouped by category and labelled by type:

- **[Industry Survey]** — Primary research conducted with sector professionals
- **[Market Report]** — Analyst or research firm market sizing and forecasting
- **[Company Statement]** — Direct statements from company executives or official channels
- **[Annual Report]** — Formal financial and operational reporting
- **[Annual Results Statement]** — Earnings releases and results announcements
- **[Press Release]** — Official company announcements
- **[Company Blog]** — Company-published editorial or insight content
- **[Company Page]** — Static informational pages from company websites
- **[Case Study / Operational Reporting]** — Documented deployment outcomes, attributed to named organisations
- **[Trade Publication]** — Third-party editorial coverage of industry developments
- **[Government / Regulatory Source]** — Official EU, national, or intergovernmental publications
- **[Aggregated Research]** — Secondary compilations of multiple research sources
- **[Industry Guide]** — Practitioner-facing overviews and analysis
- **[Dataset]** — Public datasets used for data visualisation or capability demonstration

Reliability note: [Annual Report], [Government / Regulatory Source], and [Industry Survey] sources carry the highest evidentiary weight. [Company Blog] and [Company Page] sources reflect self-reported data and should be treated as directionally indicative. [Aggregated Research] sources consolidate multiple studies — original sources are cited where available.

**Datasets removed from earlier versions of this project:**
The following Kaggle and Hugging Face datasets were evaluated during the research phase and removed after data quality review found no defensible signal supporting the dashboard story. They are listed here for audit trail purposes only and are not used in any deliverable.

| Dataset | Reason removed |
|---|---|
| Cross-Border Trade & Customs Delay (Kaggle — ziya07) | Document status showed no correlation with delay days — dataset was synthetically generated without that relationship |
| Logistics & Supply Chain Operations (Kaggle — datasetengineer) | Fulfillment label was uniform across all rows; clearance time was a scaled index with no real-world unit |
| Supply Chain Shipment Pricing / USAID (Kaggle — apoorvwatsky) | Pharmaceutical procurement data; commodity types and corridors do not map to Müller's business |
| High Quality Invoice Images for OCR (Hugging Face — Voxel51) | Not used in final story |
| Invoice Annotation Dataset (Hugging Face — longmaodata) | Not used in final story |
| SROIE Scanned Receipts (Hugging Face — darentang) | Not used in final story |
| FUNSD Form Understanding (Hugging Face — nielsr) | Not used in final story |
